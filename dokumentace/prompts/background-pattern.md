## Prompt (kopíruj celé)

> Match the style of the attached "The Early Bowl" logo. Seamless repeating tile pattern, hand-drawn cartoon clipart style with thin warm brown outlines (#7A5843). Scattered small breakfast doodles evenly spread: strawberries, blueberries, banana slices, oat/granola clusters and little leaves. Very light and low-contrast, drawn softly over a cream #FAF6EE background so it works as a subtle decorative texture behind text. Even spacing, no large empty gaps, edges must tile seamlessly when repeated.
>
> Muted warm palette only (cream, soft strawberry red, blueberry blue, honey yellow, leaf green), all desaturated and pale. No text, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no neon colors, no strong shadows, nothing that would reduce text legibility on top.
