**Reference:** přilož `assets/Theearlybowlmiska.png` — to je **zdroj pravdy**, nové varianty se mu musí podobat.

Keep proportions and colors identical to the reference. Transparent background, no extra background elements. Verify the wording reads exactly "THE EARLY BOWL".

## B) Horizontální lockup (do hlavičky)

Balanced, clean, with even padding. Transparent background. Verify the wording reads exactly "THE EARLY BOWL", no other text.
